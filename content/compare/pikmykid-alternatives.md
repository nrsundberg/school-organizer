# PikMyKid Alternatives: How School Dismissal Tools Compare in 2026

*PickupRoster publishes this page, and our product is one of the options below. We describe every tool from its own public pages or a cited third-party listing, and we note what each one does well.*

## What changed with PikMyKid

- **April 7, 2026:** school-safety company CENTEGIX announced it had acquired PikMyKid (styled "Pikmykid") and said it would begin integrating PikMyKid's capabilities into the CENTEGIX Safety Platform right away. <!-- source: https://www.centegix.com/press-release/centegix-announces-acquisition-of-pikmykid-bringing-together-leaders-in-emergency-response-and-daily-safety-operations/ (checked 2026-10-18) -->
- **October 7, 2026:** CENTEGIX brought PikMyKid under the CENTEGIX brand, with dismissal offered as part of its expanded Safety Platform alongside visitor management, attendance, digital hall passes, school messaging, wearable staff alerts and reunification. <!-- source: https://www.centegix.com/press-release/centegixs-expanded-safety-platform-supports-k-12-districts-full-range-of-safety-needs-from-daily-operations-to-critical-incidents (checked 2026-10-18) --> The dismissal product now appears on CENTEGIX's site as CENTEGIX Dismissal. <!-- source: https://www.centegix.com/dismissal and https://www.centegix.com/pikmykid (checked 2026-10-18) -->
- **For current customers:** PikMyKid's FAQ says there will be "no immediate changes" to pricing or contract terms during the transition. <!-- source: https://www.pikmykid.com/faq (checked 2026-10-18) -->

A change like this is a natural time to review your options. This page sets out what PikMyKid offers and how the alternatives compare, so you can pick what fits your school.

## What PikMyKid offers

- **Three dismissal packages.** PikMyKid's comparison page lists Free Car Line, Dismissal Lite and Dismissal Full. <!-- source: https://www.pikmykid.com/solutions/free-car-line-invitation (checked 2026-10-18) -->
  - **Free Car Line:** a web-based school portal, manual student entry, 5 free staff users, a daily dismissal report by email, and ongoing staff support. <!-- source: https://www.pikmykid.com/solutions/free-car-line-invitation (checked 2026-10-18) -->
  - **Dismissal Lite:** adds roster upload or SIS integration, unlimited staff users, customized reports with an audit trail, car line queueing, car tag management and printing, and bus, after-school and walker/bike check-in and release. <!-- source: https://www.pikmykid.com/solutions/free-car-line-invitation (checked 2026-10-18) -->
  - **Dismissal Full:** adds the parent app, car line queue automation, unlimited parent messaging, real-time parent pickup changes, dismissal notifications, a carpool module and emergency parent/child reunification. <!-- source: https://www.pikmykid.com/solutions/free-car-line-invitation (checked 2026-10-18) -->
- **Free for parents.** The parent app is free for families. <!-- source: https://www.pikmykid.com/faq (checked 2026-10-18) -->
- **A wider safety suite.** PikMyKid also offers a digital hall pass and visitor management. <!-- source: https://www.pikmykid.com/ (checked 2026-10-18) -->
- **Pricing.** Paid packages are quoted: PikMyKid sends a cost estimate after a consultation. <!-- source: https://www.pikmykid.com/faq (checked 2026-10-18) --> GetApp lists a starting price of $3,750 per year and notes a free version. That figure comes from GetApp, not from a PikMyKid price list. <!-- source: https://www.getapp.com/education-childcare-software/a/pikmykid/ (checked 2026-10-18; GetApp page says last updated September 2026) -->

**Who PikMyKid suits best:** schools and districts that want dismissal, reunification, visitor management and emergency response from one safety vendor.

## Why schools look at alternatives

- They want a **focused dismissal tool** rather than a full safety suite.
- They're **reviewing vendors after the acquisition**.
- They want a **live board** that classrooms and hallways can see, not only a staff app.

## The alternatives, briefly

1. **PickupRoster: a live, browser-based pickup board with published pricing.**
   - Each school gets its own address (yourslug.pickuproster.com). Staff call cars from a phone or tablet, and every classroom, hallway and staff screen updates at the same moment.
   - Viewer PINs and magic links are limited to your school and lock after failed attempts. English and Spanish come with every plan.
   - Drill tools sit on the same system: 12 starter templates (fire evacuation, lockdown, severe weather and more) with a live roll and an after-action report.
   - 30-day free trial with no credit card, plus two free 30-minute intro calls to set up the system.
   - *Consider another option if* you need one vendor for visitor management, hall passes and emergency alerting.
2. **QManager: automatic parent check-in by geofence.**
   - Parents' phones share their arrival when they enter the school's geofence, and staff see cars in order on an iPad or browser. <!-- source: https://dismissalsoftware.com/pricing/ (checked 2026-10-18) -->
   - Plans start at $995 for the first year (usually $1,295), with a 30-day trial and no credit card. Serving schools since 2011. <!-- source: https://dismissalsoftware.com/pricing/ (checked 2026-10-18) -->
   - Works best when families use the parent app.
3. **Schoolhouse Driveline: a simple, staff-only classroom display.**
   - Staff enter Family ID numbers from car placards, and student names show on classroom smartboards, projectors or any browser, with up to nine color-coded pickup locations. <!-- source: https://schoolhousedriveline.com/ (checked 2026-10-18) -->
   - $748 for the first year (including a 60-day free trial), then $935 a year per campus. No parent app, by design. <!-- source: https://schoolhousedriveline.com/ (checked 2026-10-18) -->
4. **Carline Hound: a low-cost staff queue.**
   - A line walker enters hang-tag numbers on a phone app, and a large display shows the list for the assembly area. <!-- source: https://carlinehound.com/ (checked 2026-10-18) -->
   - $33 a month or $299 a year per school, with a 30-day free trial. Not intended for parents. <!-- source: https://carlinehound.com/ (checked 2026-10-18) -->
5. **PickUp Patrol: dismissal-plan changes that flow to the front office.**
   - Parents change dismissal plans in a parent portal on a phone, tablet or computer, and a car line dispatcher notifies classrooms. <!-- source: https://www.pickuppatrol.net/ (checked 2026-10-18) -->
   - Pricing is tailored to school size, with no setup fees and a 60-day free trial. <!-- source: https://www.pickuppatrol.net/learn-more and https://www.pickuppatrol.net/ (checked 2026-10-18) -->
6. **SchoolPass (Raptor Technologies): a full campus-operations suite.** Raptor acquired SchoolPass in April 2023. It covers carline automation, attendance, visitor management and emergency tools, with optional license-plate recognition and RFID. <!-- source: https://raptortech.com/resources/news/raptor-technologies-acquires-schoolpass/ and https://www.schoolpass.com/ (checked 2026-10-18) --> See our [SchoolPass vs PickupRoster comparison](/compare/schoolpass-vs-pickuproster).
7. **Ruvna Dismissal: for independent schools.**
   - Weekly plans per student and multi-step plans (for example, an activity followed by car pickup), with an audit trail. <!-- source: https://www.ruvna.com/products/dismissal (checked 2026-10-18) -->
   - Pricing is quoted (flat rate for smaller schools, per student for larger ones), and Ruvna does not offer trials. <!-- source: https://www.ruvna.com/pricing (checked 2026-10-18) -->

## Frequently asked questions

### Is PikMyKid still available after the CENTEGIX acquisition?
Yes. CENTEGIX acquired PikMyKid in April 2026 and, in October 2026, brought it under the CENTEGIX brand as part of its Safety Platform. PikMyKid's FAQ says there are no immediate changes to existing pricing or contract terms. Contact CENTEGIX for current details.

### Does PikMyKid publish its prices?
PikMyKid offers a Free Car Line tier, and its paid packages are quoted after a consultation. GetApp lists a starting price of $3,750 per year; that's a third-party figure, not an official price list.

### What does PickupRoster cost?
Per school: Car Line is $150 a month or $1,440 a year, and Campus is $500 a month or $4,800 a year. District pricing is custom. Paying annually saves 20%.

### What's the difference between the Car Line and Campus plans?
Car Line covers up to 150 families, 400 students and 35 classrooms, with a teacher and staff viewer. Campus covers up to 300 families, 900 students and 80 classrooms, and adds the parent and family viewer app, reports and call history, drill replay, logo upload and a custom domain, dedicated migration and onboarding support, and priority support.

### Do we need hardware for PickupRoster?
No. It runs in a web browser on the screens, tablets and phones you already have. RFID vehicle-tag auto-arrival is an optional add-on for Campus and District plans, priced per deployment.

### Can we try PickupRoster first?
Yes. The free trial lasts 30 calendar days from signup, with no credit card. Every new school also gets two free 30-minute intro calls. If you don't add a payment method by the end of the trial, the site is suspended and you aren't charged.

## Choose SchoolPass if…

- You want **one vendor** for visitor management, attendance, dismissal and emergency tools.
- You want **license-plate cameras** to identify cars automatically, and you can support the installation.
- You already use other Raptor products.

## Choose PickupRoster if…

- You mainly need a **calm, visible car line**: a live board every classroom and hallway can see.
- You want to **know the price up front** and pay monthly or yearly.
- You want to **start quickly with no hardware**, and try it with your own car line before you pay.
- You also want **drill tools** on the same system.

**In use today:** a K-12 school in Maryland has used PickupRoster for over two years.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does SchoolPass cost?
SchoolPass doesn't publish prices. One public August 2022 quote to a 7-campus Pennsylvania district listed the Cloud Basic subscription at $1,540 per campus for year 1 on a 3-year term, after discounts, with visitor-management modules and hardware priced separately. Your quote will differ.

### Who owns SchoolPass?
Raptor Technologies acquired SchoolPass in April 2023.

### Does PickupRoster use license-plate cameras?
No. Staff call cars from the board. RFID vehicle-tag auto-arrival is an optional add-on for Campus and District plans, priced per deployment, and it isn't needed to run the board.

### Can PickupRoster replace SchoolPass?
For car line dismissal, dismissal plans and drills, it may. PickupRoster doesn't include visitor management, attendance automation or license-plate recognition, so schools that rely on those would need another tool.

### What does PickupRoster cost?
Per school: Car Line is $150 a month or $1,440 a year (up to 400 students), and Campus is $500 a month or $4,800 a year (up to 900 students). District pricing is custom. Annual billing saves 20%.

### Can we try PickupRoster before buying?
Yes. The free trial lasts 30 calendar days from signup with no credit card, and every new school gets two free 30-minute intro calls.
